## English

**Name (30):** Wayline: Live Traffic & Maps
**Subtitle — App Store (30):** Drive smarter with live reports
**Short description — Play (80):** Turn-by-turn navigation with live traffic and reports from drivers around you.

**Description:**

Wayline gets you there faster with live traffic measured by drivers on the road right now.

• Turn-by-turn voice guidance in English and Hebrew
• Routes ranked by live traffic, with a faster route offered while you drive
• Police, crashes, hazards, closures and speed cameras reported by drivers — confirm or dismiss with one tap
• Speed limits along your route and a warning when you go over
• Avoid tolls or highways
• Save Home and Work for one-tap routes

Built for privacy: use it without an account; traffic data is anonymous and the start and end of every trip never leave your phone.

Continued use of GPS running in the background can decrease battery life. Wayline only uses background location while you navigate.
